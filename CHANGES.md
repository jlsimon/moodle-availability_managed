# Release notes

## 1.0.0 — 19 August 2026

- Initial release of the standalone `availability_managed` plugin.
- Central dashboard for section and activity availability.
- Course, group, and individual-user access targets.
- Manual progression, bulk application, audit trail, reconciliation,
  privacy support, and course backup and restore.
- Support for Moodle 4.5 LTS and later.
